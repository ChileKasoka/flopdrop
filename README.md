💦 FlopDrop – Music App for Producers

FlopDrop is a web-based music platform designed for producers to discover, play, and collaborate on beats. Featuring the latest Chonk beats, trending tracks, and a community of top producers, FlopDrop makes music creation and discovery seamless and fun.

Features
🎵 Chonk Beats Grid

Displays the latest uploaded Chonk beats in a responsive grid.

Each beat has a play button to listen instantly.

Highlights the currently playing track.

🌟 Featured Producers

Grid or carousel showing top producers.

Includes avatars, bios, and links to their tracks.

Helps discover and connect with other producers.

🔥 Popular / Trending Beats

Shows the most played or liked beats.

Displays stats like plays and downloads.

Lets producers quickly find popular tracks.

⚡ Header & Navigation

Fixed header with logo and menu.

Menu links to Home, Beats, Producers, Tutorials, and Contact sections.

🛠️ Footer

About FlopDrop, quick links, contact info, social media links, and legal info.

Fully responsive and clean design.
